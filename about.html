About me

Academic background and current research affiliation.

Academic profile

I am a PhD student in Applied Mathematics at Universidad del Bío-Bío (UBB), in Concepción, Chile. My research focuses on Numerical Analysis, particularly the mathematical analysis and numerical simulation of coupled partial differential equation models. I am currently conducting my doctoral research under the supervision of my PhD advisor, Ricardo Oyarzúa, and my co-advisor, Giordano Tierra.

Affiliation

Universidad del Bío-Bío
Doctoral Program in Applied Mathematics · Concepción, Chile

Research group and funding

My doctoral research is associated with Project Anillo ACT210087, “Anillo of Computational Mathematics for Desalination Processes”, and is supported in part by the ANID Doctoral Scholarship – Becas Chile 2023.
